# AI Customer Retention Platform

An AI-powered B2B SaaS platform that helps organizations identify, prioritize, and retain customers at risk of churn.

## Core Promise

Identify → Prioritize → Explain → Recommend → Execute → Measure → Learn

## What the Platform Does

The platform helps organizations:

- Identify customers at risk of churn
- Prioritize customers based on risk and business value
- Understand why a customer is at risk
- Recommend the next best retention action
- Execute retention actions
- Track customer outcomes
- Measure revenue saved and retention impact
- Learn which retention strategies work best
