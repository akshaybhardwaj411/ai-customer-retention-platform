# AI Customer Retention Platform

## Product Type

B2B SaaS platform.

## Primary User

Organizations and their retention/customer-success teams.

## Problem

Organizations often know that customers are leaving only after
the customer has already decided to churn.

They need to know:

1. Which customers are at risk?
2. Which customers should be prioritized?
3. Why are they at risk?
4. What action should the organization take?
5. Who should execute the action?
6. Did the action work?
7. How much revenue was saved?

## Core Product Promise

The platform identifies at-risk customers, prioritizes them,
explains the risk, recommends the next best action, enables
the organization to execute that action, and measures the
business impact.

## Core Loop

Detect
→ Prioritize
→ Explain
→ Recommend
→ Execute
→ Measure
→ Learn

## MVP

1. Organization onboarding
2. Customer data import
3. Customer 360
4. Churn risk prediction
5. Risk prioritization
6. Action Center
7. AI Next Best Action
8. Action execution
9. Outcome tracking
10. Revenue impact
